# Video Producer: screenshots

Twenty-four images of the plugin alone, English interface: the three that the README shows, the nine that the user guide shows, and twelve from earlier versions that no page shows. In this repository every image is stored as WebP (quality 85), converted from the JPEG and PNG captures that this page describes: sizes and crops are unchanged, and the formats given below are those of the captures.

## User guide

These nine images were taken on 18 November 2026 on a development instance of PotionUI that served the `dev` branch of the fork, with Video Producer at the same commit, in the dark theme, in a browser viewport of 1440 x 900 at a device scale of 1. The five full-window images are the whole viewport; the four others are crops of it at the size of the window, drawer or popover they show, without reduction.

The project is the starter project "Lumi’s potion", made by the plugin's own code for a test account and stored on the instance under a test name, then opened in the page. Nothing was generated: the project has no take, and the engine of the project was set to MiniMax H3 (12 GB) in the **Generation settings** drawer. Two things are staged: the browser wrote "Lumi’s potion" over the test name in the project bar and in the file name of the export window, just before each capture; and the estimate is the instance's own, measured on 20 earlier takes of that preset.

## Starter project

The first seven images of the table are 720 x 450 reductions (50 %) of 1440 x 900 captures in the dark theme; the last two rows are 1440 x 900 captures.

They were taken on 11 November 2026 for version 0.2.0. The `starter-storyboard-dark`, `starter-media-dark` and `starter-director-references-dark` images were taken again later that day, once the two bottle images had become their 16:9 versions (whole bottle, blank label). The `starter-director-direction-dark` and `starter-director-bible-dark` images came after that, once the card said that Lumi is a woman in her thirties in a pointed witch's hat, shots 1 and 3 said "at night" and the props and shot 1 described the straight bottle of the images, each time for a new account of the same instance.

The first seven show the starter project "Lumi’s potion" as the page creates it for a new account on a throwaway instance of the pinned core with no GPU: four shots to generate, four prompts, the card of Lumi with her portrait and the card of the workshop, two global references, the three images in Media, the first and last images of shot 4, a FL2VA profile, no take. The page saves the project as soon as it opens, so the project bar reads "Saved at" and the time. The six Video Producer presets were installed and assigned to the account, and the model files were mounted read-only from another instance, so the estimates are the server's own.

One thing is staged: with no GPU, the plugin lists no engine (a native backend configured for the CPU is left out), so the browser added the native backend of the instance to the engines of `GET /me` before the page read them; without that, the Production bar reads "No engine". The default engine is the first video preset of the instance, MiniMax H3 (12 GB). Shot prompts, cards and the direction are in English.

## Takes, final render, engine and estimate

These five images, PNG captures, were retaken on 12 November 2026 for version 0.3.1, with test accounts. Each joins the dark theme (left) and the light theme (right), as 1:1 crops of larger captures (no reduction).

`takes-cards.webp` and `take-labels.webp` come from a throwaway instance without a GPU that served `dev` and worked on a copy of the storage of two earlier GPU test instances. Nothing in them is staged: the takes are real MiniMax-H3 (12 GB) drafts made on those instances (two of them failed there for real, with the `gpu_restart_required` code), the shots were renamed in English in the copy, and the labels were set by clicks.

`make-final.webp`, `engine-popup.webp` and `estimate-sound.webp` come from a throwaway instance that served `dev` with the GPU visible, on a copy of the storage of an earlier GPU test instance with "Lumi’s potion" and its two real MiniMax-H3 (12 GB) drafts; no take was generated.
